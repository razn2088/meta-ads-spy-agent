## Colours
| Role | Hex | RGB |
|---|---|---|
| Night navy (primary dark) | `#0E0B26` | 14, 11, 38 |
| Navy (primary) | `#282C51` | 40, 44, 81 |
| Orange (secondary) | `#F28D35` | 242, 141, 53 |
| Yellow (secondary) | `#FAED64` | 250, 237, 100 |
| Off-white | `#F7F7F7` | 247, 247, 247 |

- **Signature gradient:** yellow → orange (`#FAED64 → #F28D35`), used for accents, arrows, highlights.
- Navy + off-white carry the design; orange/yellow are **secondary** accents (buttons, key words, arrows, charts) — never large text on white in pure yellow.
- **Motif:** the upward arrow (straight or curved), in the yellow→orange gradient — "aiming high".
